$ evnx vault revoke --help
Revoke a member's access, rotating the vault key.

⚠️ This re-encrypts every version of the vault under a fresh key and re-wraps it for everyone who stays. It is not a quick operation and it is not reversible.

What it achieves: the removed member can no longer read anything pushed from now on. What it cannot: recall copies of what they could already read. Rotate those secrets at their source.

Usage: evnx vault revoke [OPTIONS] --user <USER> <TARGET>

Arguments:
  <TARGET>
          Vault: `name`, `name/environment`, or an id

Options:
      --user <USER>
          Email address of the member to remove

  -y, --yes
          Skip the confirmation prompt

      --no-rekey
          Remove the member WITHOUT rotating the vault key.
          
          ⚠️ They keep the ability to decrypt every version they already had access to, including ones pushed after this. Only use it when you are re-keying separately, or when the member never held the key.

      --password-stdin
          Read the master password from stdin instead of prompting

      --server <URL>
          evnx server to talk to. Overrides EVNX_SERVER and config.toml

  -v, --verbose
          Enable verbose output

  -q, --quiet
          Suppress non-essential output

      --no-color
          Disable colored output

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

  -V, --version
          Print version

[exit 0]
